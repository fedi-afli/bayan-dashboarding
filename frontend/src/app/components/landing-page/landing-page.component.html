What it does with your data

Four things happen automatically between the file you upload and the chart you read.

Upload anything tabular

Bring a CSV, an Excel export, or raw JSON. Columns are recognized automatically — in English, French or Arabic — you just confirm with one click.

Automatic counts & aggregates

Totals, averages, and group-bys are computed for you the moment the file lands — no formulas, no pivot tables.

Charts, picked automatically

The shape of your data decides the chart — trends become lines, comparisons become bars, shares become proportions.

Built for the decision, not the report

Every dashboard is meant to answer a question fast, so you can act on it, not just admire it.
